--- NEXT (Iteration 3 — behavior-based item embeddings) ---

[ ] Plan C: replace tabular features with item2vec embeddings

    Motivation: RQ-VAE needs rich continuous geometry. Tabular features
    (category/price/name) are too flat. Collaborative filtering embeddings
    capture what items are bought/viewed together — actual semantic structure.

    Implementation:
    1. Train item2vec (Word2Vec skip-gram via gensim) on session sequences
       from training data. Treat each session as a sentence of SKU IDs.
       Output: 64-dim embedding per SKU.

    2. Replace ItemFeatureEncoder in ingestion/rqvae.py with a simple
       Linear(64, latent_dim) that takes item2vec embeddings as input.

    3. Re-run: python train.py --rebuild
       Expect: much higher unique triple count (approaching 128^3 coverage),
       lower collision rate, better item diversity in generated sessions.

    Thesis framing:
    "Items are represented by behavior-derived semantic IDs capturing
     co-purchase similarity, obtained by applying RQ-VAE to item2vec
     embeddings trained on real session sequences."

    References: item2vec (Barkan & Koenigstein, 2016), TIGER (Rajput et al.,
    NeurIPS 2023), MBGen (WWW 2024)
